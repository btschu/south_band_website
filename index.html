About Us

The Cheyenne South High School Band is a community where every student belongs. We work hard, support each other, and build strong musicians and confident, responsible people along the way.

Whether you're a current member, thinking about joining, or cheering us on from the stands, we're glad you're here.

Our Ensembles

Many ensembles, one band family

Marching Band

Performs at football games and parades and competes at the State Marching Festival. Since State became a competition, the band has placed 2nd both years. It has also earned superior ratings and caption awards for music and marching.

Concert Band

Plays challenging music and consistently earns superior ratings at the Wyoming Music Performance Assessments (MPAs).

Jazz Bands

Two jazz bands that perform at concerts and regional jazz festivals, where they consistently earn superior ratings.

Percussion Ensemble

Our percussionists play demanding music on everything from drums to mallet instruments. The ensemble is consistently strong and earns superior ratings year after year.

Pep Band

Plays at games and pep rallies, keeping the crowd loud and school spirit high.
